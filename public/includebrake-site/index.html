Our proof rule

The Baseline Rule: We Measure Before We Build

We do not sell theories or hype. Before we write a single line of logic, we record how long your current process takes by the clock. After the fix is deployed, we time it again. If the new method does not measurably outperform the old one, we do not bill you for expansion. You maintain full oversight at every step.
