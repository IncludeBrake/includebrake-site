FAQ

Every objection.
Answered straight.

Will this replace my receptionist?

No. The bot handles after-hours and overflow — your receptionist still owns relationships and complex calls. We're adding a second shift, not eliminating the first.

What if the bot gives wrong information?

It only answers from the knowledge base we build together. Anything outside that scope gets escalated to you. It cannot fabricate answers.

Will customers know it's AI?

Your call. We can make it transparent ("I'm an AI assistant") or natural ("Hi, I'm here to help"). We set it up the way that fits your business.

What does it cost to run after setup?

Roughly $10–30/month in tool costs depending on your traffic. We walk you through that at handoff. No per-conversation charges, no markup.

What's my ROI?

One missed lead/week at a $300 average job = $15,600/year. The bot pays for itself in under a month — before counting improved customer experience.

Do I need to be technical?

Zero. We handle everything. Your web person pastes one line of code. That's the full ask.
