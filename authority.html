The Intake Process

What you send.
Why it matters.

After payment, you get an automated email with two inputs. The sooner I have them, the more calibration time before your slot.

Input 1
Voice Analysis Samples

Upload 3 PDFs of your best emails, blog posts, or talk transcripts. OR link a podcast/video episode you've done.

Important: emails, Slack rants, voice notes — NOT polished LinkedIn posts. Polished posts are already filtered; they strip the voice you need.

Input 2
The 10-Minute Rant

A Loom or voice note answering three prompts: What's the biggest lie in your industry? What's a recent client win and how did you get it? What are you selling right now and who is it for?

If your recording is under 4 minutes, redo it. The first 2 minutes are always PR spin. I need what comes after.
